## Grammar and gate

Custom grammar: **assembly bench**. Navigation indexes three visitor tasks (approach, dashboards, build). Opening combines editorial copy with real product planes. Quiet origin precedes the one pinned transformation. A selectable contact sheet and trust evidence follow; the close is a working repository handoff. Bans: full-screen video, scrub clips, fake live dashboards, repeated pins, feature-card grids, generated screen imagery and auth-gated demo CTAs.

The eight stock grammars were evaluated: filmic one-shot overweights video; chaptered editorial forbids the product-led opening; live surface would imply these screenshots are running software; continuous world imposes an unbroken flight; typographic poster underplays the real product captures; gallery/catalog alone under-explains the architectural turn; split stage fixes columns through a story needing a central assembly; rhythmic cutlist bans the pinned transformation and makes the story too hurried. This grammar instead constrains navigation, sequence, ending and bans around the shared-host explanation.

Registry gate: first build, no existing rows to compare. No gate bypass.
